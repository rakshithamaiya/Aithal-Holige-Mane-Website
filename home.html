About Us

Aithal Holige Mane is a holige plus condiments shop. Ask us what's not available here!. From sweet items like holige, ladu, jamoon, chikkis, halkovas, badam puri, etc to savoury items like chakli, nippat, chips, nippat, grains, etc to pickles of all variety. We also sell festive items like sugar-molds, kobri designs, etc.
